TALK TILES — Privacy Policy

Effective 18 September 2026. Applies to Talk Tiles for Android (com.talktiles.app) and Talk Tiles for iPad.

The short version

Talk Tiles is a picture-communication (AAC) app that works entirely on your device. It has no account, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no server. Nothing you put into it — pages, buttons, photos, voice recordings, settings — ever leaves the device unless you choose to share or back it up yourself.

What the app stores, and where

  • Your communication book (pages, buttons, words, colours) is saved in the app's private storage on the device.
  • Photos you take with the camera or choose from your library are copied into the book and stored in the same private storage. The original photo in your library is not changed or uploaded.
  • Voice recordings you make for a button are stored in the same private storage.
  • Settings (voice, speed, touch options, child-lock PIN) are stored in the same private storage. The PIN is stored as plain text; it exists to stop a child opening the editor, not to protect data.

Uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

Permissions

  • Microphone — only while you are recording your own voice for a button. Nothing is recorded at any other time.
  • Camera — only when you tap "Take Photo with Camera" for a button or scene.

Both are optional; every part of the app works without them.

Speech

Words are spoken either from recordings bundled inside the app ("Bella") or by your device's own text-to-speech engine. Text is not sent to any Talk Tiles server. If you choose an online voice from your device's text-to-speech settings, that engine's own privacy policy applies.

Sharing and backups

"Back Up Everything" and "Share This Page" create a file and hand it to the app you pick (email, messages, files, cloud storage). That file contains your book, including photos and recordings. Where it goes from there is up to you and the app you share it with; Talk Tiles never receives a copy.

Children

Talk Tiles is used by children with the help of a parent, teacher or therapist. The app collects no personal information from anyone, of any age, and shows no advertising.

Data we collect

None. There is no account, no analytics, no crash reporting and no network connection made by the app.
